# 30-Second Demonstration Video Script — PyroNexa 26

**Project:** PyroNexa – Satellite Fire Intelligence Platform  
**Challenge:** 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge — Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots  
**Target Duration:** Exactly 30 seconds  

---

## Storyboard & Narration

| Timecode | Visual Display | Spoken Narration (Voiceover) |
|---|---|---|
| **00:00 – 00:05** | **Opening Title**: PyroNexa 26 logo, switching to the Live Overview showing KPI cards (Total Hotspots, MODIS vs VIIRS counts, FRP, and footprint area). | *"Wildfire response demands historical clarity, but raw NASA MODIS and VIIRS satellite fire feeds cannot simply be summed."* |
| **00:05 – 00:11** | **Live Fire Map**: Screen shows California fire cluster; user toggles between blue MODIS (1km) circles and orange VIIRS (375m) circles, opening a popup with telemetry. | *"VIIRS detects nearly four times more pixels than MODIS. PyroNexa normalizes ground footprint area and resolves sub-pixel burning complexes."* |
| **00:11 – 00:18** | **Burning Activity Calendar**: Camera sweeps across the Year-vs-Month heatmap matrix, hovering over peak burning months and historical anomaly years. | *"Our Burning Activity Calendar reveals decadal seasonal cycles, instantly distinguishing normal fire seasons from severe climate surges."* |
| **00:18 – 00:25** | **Harmonization & Anomalies**: Fast cut to Sensor Comparison RFAI curves, followed by the Statistical Anomaly engine highlighting extreme events ($Z \ge 3.0$). | *"With dimensionless Relative Fire Activity indices and robust Z-score anomaly scoring, historical records become truly comparable."* |
| **00:25 – 00:30** | **Data Explorer & Call to Action**: One-click CSV download, ending on the PyroNexa team closing card with GitHub link. | *"PyroNexa: Transparent, scientifically responsible fire intelligence for our warming planet."* |
